KORD Community Rules

Draft. Have a lawyer review this before the app is released on the App Store.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

These rules cover everything you post on KORD: ratings, comments, photos and posts on The Wire. You agree to them before your first post.

The rules

  1. Be honest. Post about food you actually ate and places you actually went.
  2. Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying, including of restaurant staff.
  3. Keep it clean. Nothing sexual, violent or illegal.
  4. No spam, ads or fake reviews. Do not post for a restaurant you work for or are paid by without saying so.
  5. Only post photos you took yourself, with no people in them unless they said yes.
  6. No private information about anyone.

Reporting and blocking

Every post, comment and photo has a Report link. Reported items are hidden for you at once and are reviewed by KORD. You can block any member from their page or from one of their posts; you will no longer see their posts and they can no longer follow you.

What happens when rules are broken

KORD may hide or remove anything that breaks these rules and may suspend or remove accounts that keep breaking them.

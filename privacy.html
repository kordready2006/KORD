KORD Privacy Policy

Draft. Have a lawyer review this before the app is released on the App Store.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

What KORD is

KORD shows food deals near college campuses and lets students rate and comment on what they ate.

What is stored on your phone

Your chosen school, name, profile photo, ratings, comments, demo orders, points and rewards are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. Clearing your browser or app data removes them.

What is sent to our server

When shared ratings are switched on, KORD sends the following to a database hosted by Supabase so that other students can see them:

  • your star ratings for dishes;
  • photos of dishes you choose to add (they are shrunk on your phone first, which removes location data), stored in Supabase file storage;
  • the comments you post, with the name you entered (or "Student" if you entered none);
  • your account: the email address, display name and password you sign up with. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth; your password is stored only in hashed form and KORD cannot read it;
  • your account ID, used to keep one rating per dish per account and to enforce the 12-hour rating limit;
  • a count of taps on a restaurant's Order or Website button.

Comments and dish photos are public to everyone using KORD at your school. Do not put private information in a comment or a photo, and do not post photos of people. Your profile photo is never uploaded.

What KORD does not collect

Browsing needs no account. KORD does not ask for your phone number, student ID, precise location or payment details. Your email is used only to sign you in, verify your account and reset your password. Ordering in the current version is a demonstration only: no payment is taken and no order is sent to a restaurant.

Other services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are requested. Links to restaurant websites and maps open those sites, which have their own privacy policies. KORD adds "utm_source=kord" to links so a restaurant can see that a visit came from KORD.

Children

KORD is meant for college students and is not directed at children under 13.

Removing your data

Clear the app's data on your device to remove everything stored there. You can delete your account at any time from the You tab; this permanently removes your login, ratings, comments and photos from the shared server. To have a single rating, comment or photo removed from the shared server, email us with the restaurant, dish and the text of the comment.

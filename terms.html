Deals can change

KORD collects deals from restaurants' websites, school discount pages and local publications, and links each one to its source. Restaurants change or end deals without notice. KORD does not guarantee that any deal, price, menu item or opening time is current. Confirm with the restaurant before you go.

Not affiliated

KORD is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any restaurant, chain, college or university shown in the app. Their names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Photos

Unless a restaurant has given KORD its own pictures, food photos are examples of a kind of food and do not show the restaurant's actual dish. Photo credits are listed in the app under Sources and credits.

Ordering, points and KORD Plus

Ordering, points, rewards and the KORD Plus membership in the current version are demonstrations. No money is charged, no order reaches a restaurant, and points and rewards have no cash value.

Ratings and comments

Be honest and respectful. Do not post anything unlawful, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit or private about another person, and do not post spam or advertising. KORD may hide or remove any comment and may block a device that abuses the service. By posting, you allow KORD to display what you post in the app.

Alcohol

KORD lists food deals. Some listed places also serve alcohol; you must be 21 or older to buy or drink alcohol in Ohio.

No warranty

KORD is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, KORD is not liable for losses that come from relying on information in the app.

Restaurants

If you own or manage a listed restaurant and want a listing corrected or removed, or want to supply your own photos and logo, email kordready2006@gmail.com.
